# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Listeners, musicians and audio professionals separating songs at their desks, often in dim studios, with headphones and an existing local music collection.

## Product Purpose

Make excellent local stem separation an ordinary desktop workflow: import, choose a target and quality, separate, listen and export. Advanced users retain exact model selection, ensembles and inference controls.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, approachable. Temporary product name: Separator.

## Anti-references

No generic dashboard cards, decorative gradients, glass panels, fake meters or terminal requirements. Raycast's compact interaction, Linear's hierarchy and professional audio software's transport are references, without cloning them.

## Design Principles

- Audio quality and truthful progress come first.
- A small number of clear choices precede advanced controls.
- Model identity and device use are always inspectable.
- Audio and user outputs belong to the user and stay local.
- Every visible control has a working, discoverable outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard workflow, semantic controls, visible focus, readable contrast, labelled audio transport and reduced motion. English first; strings are kept separate from engine contracts.
